# Alpine opening assets

Surface photographs (CC0), Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven:
- Rock 01: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_01 — diffuse and bump, 1K JPG sources.
- Snow 02: https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_02 — bump, 1K JPG source.
- License: https://polyhaven.com/license

Textures re-encoded to WebP quality 88. Original file names retained for provenance.
The 401×401 little-endian unsigned 16-bit heightfield `massif.u16` is generated
by `scripts/build-alpine-assets.py`: a composed fictional massif, not surveyed
terrain or a downloaded scan. X spans -700…700 and Z -650…650; height = sample/65535*512.
